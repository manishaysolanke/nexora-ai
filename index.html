What is Nexora AI?+

Nexora AI is a productivity platform combining AI assistance, document intelligence, writing tools, tasks, analytics, and team collaboration in one workspace.

Is there a free plan?+

Yes. The Free plan costs ₹0/month and includes 50 AI requests per month, 3 document uploads, a personal workspace, and basic AI writing.

Can I upload documents?+

Yes. Nexora AI supports document uploads for users who want to ask questions and work with their information.

What is the difference between Pro and Business?+

Pro is designed for individual creators and power users. Business adds team projects, higher AI usage, team workspace features, and admin controls.

Can I book a demo?+

Yes. Use the Book a Demo form on this website and submit your contact details and preferred time.
